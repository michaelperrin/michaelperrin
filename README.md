# Hi, I'm Michaël 👋

I'm a freelance web developer based in Paris. I build business web applications, the tools people use all day at work, with a focus on clear interfaces and front-end code that stays maintainable over time.

I'm curious by nature. I like to understand how things work, whether it's a tangled codebase or the business my software is meant to serve. I lived and worked in Houston, Texas, for a few years, then worked remotely with a team based in Amsterdam, and I enjoy that mix of perspectives.

Away from the keyboard, you'll usually find me on a trail. I love hiking, ideally starting from a train station with no car needed, sometimes over several days with a tent. That passion became [RandoNavigo](#side-project), my side project. I also run a few times a week.

**Available for freelance missions:** remote, or on site in the Paris area (Île-de-France).

## What I do

- **Business apps & SaaS**: data-heavy screens, tables, forms, dashboards
- **Redesigns and migrations** of existing applications, without breaking what already works
- **Front-end architecture** and code quality on long-lived projects
